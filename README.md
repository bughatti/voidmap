# VoidMap - Movable World Map

**Works on:** WoW Midnight · WoW Forever

**The world map in a window of its own: everything Blizzard's map does, in the Void look, with your quests beside it.**

VoidMap gives the world map its own window. You keep the full navigation, the filters, the zoom, and every pin from Blizzard and from your other addons. The window, the buttons and the quest list are VoidMap's own.

---

### Same map, new window
- **World / Continent / Zone navigation** across the top. Click a level to go there, or click its arrow to pick another map at that level. A **Floor** picker shows up on maps with levels (dungeons, cities)
- **Filter** and **Pin**: Blizzard's own map filters (quest objectives, levels, tracked items and the rest) and map-pin placement, in VoidMap's toolbar
- **Zoom** buttons, plus the mouse wheel and drag to pan as usual
- **Every pin stays:** quests, flight paths, dungeon entrances, rares, and the pins of your other map addons
- **M**, **L** and **Esc** work as always

### Your quests beside the map
- **On this map** first: the quests whose objectives are on the map you're looking at. Then the rest of your log under its zones
- Coloured by level, with progress on the right and a mark on the quest you're tracking
- **Hover** for the objectives (and the quest lights up on the map), **click** to read the whole quest (objectives, full text, rewards, with Track, Share and Abandon), **ctrl-click** to track it, **shift-click** to link it in chat, **right-click** to stop tracking it
- The **arrow tab** on the map's right edge (or the **Quests** button) folds the list away, like Blizzard's map

### Your window, your way
- **Drag the title bar** to move it; **drag the corner** to size it. The map keeps its shape, so it always fills the window
- **Big** fills most of the screen, **Small** goes back to your size
- **Scale** and **map opacity** (see the world through the map), Blizzard's **fade while moving**
- **Coordinates** for you and the cursor at the bottom

### Explore Azeroth (WoW Forever)
- **Explore** in the title bar shows your progress toward **Explore Azeroth**, which gives a Legacy point: zones explored per continent, the areas of the zone you're looking at that you haven't discovered (click one for the way there), and every zone of the continent with its count (click to open its map)
- While it's open, the map marks each area you haven't discovered yet

### Trails (for VoidGPS)
- VoidMap records the paths you walk and ride (not flying, taxis, boats or instances), for all your characters. **VoidGPS** will use them to plan walking routes around mountains and cliffs instead of in straight lines
- The count is at the bottom right of the map. It stays on your computer, and you can turn it off in Settings

### Settings
- The **Settings** button on the map, the **Void hub icon** by the minimap, or `/vmap`. Settings are per character

---

## Compatibility

- **WoW Midnight** 12.1.5 (Season 2)
- **WoW Forever**
- Standalone: nothing else to install
- Works in combat: the world map has no protected parts, so the window never blocks an action
- With **TomTom**, VoidMap hides TomTom's coordinates on the map, because VoidMap shows its own at the bottom. TomTom's waypoints and arrow are unchanged
